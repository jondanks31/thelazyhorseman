How it works

For the yard

  • Add what you have got: indoor, outdoor, horsewalker, lunge pen, gallops.
  • Set the hours, the slot length, and how far ahead people can book.
  • Approve riders yourself, or paste in thirty email addresses and invite them.
  • Block out a clinic or a farrier day and everyone sees it straight away.
  • See who booked what, and who keeps not turning up.

For your riders

  • Their own login, not one shared around the yard.
  • What is free today, on their phone, without asking anybody.
  • Book a slot, or cancel one, in about four seconds.
  • Book straight from WhatsApp if they would rather.
  • Nothing to install and nothing to pay.

They can book from WhatsApp

Your yard already runs on WhatsApp, so the booking can happen there too. Pin one link in the group and a tap opens a private chat with the booking line. It lists what is free, they pick a slot, and that is the booking made.

The bot cannot sit in the group itself, because WhatsApp does not let businesses do that. The pinned link is the way in.

It runs on its own

Your yard gets its own address

manorfarm.thelazyhorseman.com

Riders go straight there. They save it to their phone, add it to their home screen if they want an icon, and book without ever passing through us. Put it on the noticeboard and on the gate.

Nobody has to make an account with The Lazy Horseman to use your yard's booking. It is your system, at your address, and it works on its own.
